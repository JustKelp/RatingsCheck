How to Play
Guess the hidden player in 5 tries. Use the sport selector in the top right to switch between NBA 2K, NHL 26, Madden 26, and MLB The Show 26.
After each wrong guess, one more attribute is revealed. The first 3 attributes are shown at the start.
Each tile shows how close that attribute is between your guess and the answer. Arrows show whether the answer is higher ▲ or lower ▼.
87
Exact — exact match
74

61
Warm — within 7 points
42
Cold — more than 7 points away
Each guess also shows two hint chips for position and team:
PG
Green — exact position or exact team
SF
Yellow — same position group or same conference
C
Black — different group or different conference
Position groups: NBA (guard/forward/center) · NHL (forward/defense) · NFL (offense/defense) · MLB (pitcher/infield/outfield)
Team hint: green = exact team · yellow = same division · red = same conference · black = different conference
